## Summary

This strand tracks sources that place language models in broader cognitive, epistemic, or SSH contexts. It is not yet a full active cluster, but the wiki already has several bridge sources that make the strand visible.

## Scope

The strand includes work on language and thought, disciplinary meanings of understanding, epistemic interpretation of model behavior, and the use of language models in wider cultural or scholarly settings.

It should stay broader than pure semantics, but narrower than generic commentary about AI.

## Central Questions

- How do different disciplines frame language understanding, thought, and cognition?
- Which uses of language models depend on methodological caution rather than strong anthropomorphic claims?
- What kinds of cultural or SSH interpretation are supported by the present evidence?
- Which bridge sources best connect language-centered evidence to wider cognitive and epistemic questions?

## Principal Subtopics

- language versus thought
- disciplinary meanings of understanding
- derivative meaning and limited agency
- world-model and reasoning interpretations
- SSH-facing methodological reflection

## Major Positions or Debates

- whether LLMs are merely formal linguistic systems or partial models of broader cognition
- whether language understanding should be defined behaviorally, mechanistically, or disciplinarily
- whether LLMs are better viewed as tools, libraries, agents, or constrained epistemic systems

## Gaps and Next Priorities

- SSH-method and cultural-interpretive material remains much thinner than cognition-facing bridge material
- there is not yet a dedicated page structure for qualitative or humanities use-cases
- repository evidence for explicitly cultural framing is thinner than evidence for language/cognition framing
- the next deep ingests for this strand should probably come from already indexed bridge sources rather than from broad cognitive-science background volumes
