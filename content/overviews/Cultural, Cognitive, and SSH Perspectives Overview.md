## Summary

This strand tracks sources that place language models in broader cognitive, social, cultural, and interpretive contexts. Its role is to organize bridge material that matters for WoLaLa’s foundations without pretending that cognition, SSH method, cultural variation, and human-machine comparison are one undifferentiated literature.

## Scope

The strand includes work on language and thought, disciplinary meanings of understanding, social and communicative structure, cultural and linguistic variation, epistemic interpretation of model behavior, and the use of language models in wider scholarly settings.

It should stay broader than pure semantics, but narrower than generic commentary about AI.

## Central Questions

- How do different disciplines frame language understanding, thought, and cognition?
- Which uses of language models depend on methodological caution rather than strong anthropomorphic claims?
- What kinds of cultural or SSH interpretation are supported by the present evidence?
- Which bridge sources best connect language-centered evidence to wider cognitive and epistemic questions?

## Principal Subtopics

- language, thought, and cognitive architecture
- human versus machine understanding
- social and communicative structure
- cultural and crosslinguistic variation
- SSH-facing interpretation and research responsibility

## Major Positions or Debates

- whether LLMs are merely formal linguistic systems or partial models of broader cognition
- whether language understanding should be defined behaviorally, mechanistically, or disciplinarily
- whether LLMs are better viewed as tools, libraries, agents, or constrained epistemic systems
- whether social and cultural structure should be treated as constitutive evidence rather than downstream application context

- the next deep ingests for this strand should probably come from already indexed bridge sources rather than from broad cognitive-science background volumes
- this strand still needs stronger internal linkage among cognitive architecture, social communication, and SSH responsibility, but it no longer reads as one undifferentiated list
