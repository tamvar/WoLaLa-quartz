## Summary

This strand is intentionally skeletal. The repository contains a few bridge items relevant to neuroscience and cognition, but not yet a mature neuroscience-facing source cluster.

## Scope

The strand covers neuroscientific comparison, representational similarity, neurocognitive interpretation, language and brain function, and the limits of biological-plausibility arguments where they matter for WoLaLa.

## Central Questions

- When do language-model results genuinely inform neuroscientific questions?
- Which current bridge sources help separate linguistic competence from broader cognitive or brain-level claims?
- What kinds of neuroscience-facing evidence are missing from the present repository?

## Principal Subtopics

- language versus thought distinctions
- cognitive-neuroscience comparison
- representation and mixed selectivity
- biologically informed critiques of language-model interpretation

## Major Positions or Debates

- whether LLMs should be treated as cognitive or neural models at all
- whether formal linguistic success tells us anything about brain-like language processing
