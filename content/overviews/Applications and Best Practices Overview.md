## Summary

This strand is intentionally skeletal. In WoLaLa, it should focus on language-oriented scholarly and methodological practice rather than generic AI deployment or infrastructure.

## Scope

The strand covers how language models are used in language-focused research, including linguistic analysis, annotation, qualitative or SSH workflows, evaluation design, reproducibility, prompt methodology, and responsible scholarly use.

It does not treat generic engineering, serving, safety-ops, or agent-infrastructure material as adequate substitutes.

## Central Questions

- How should language-focused scholarship use LLMs responsibly and reproducibly?
- Which application cases genuinely illuminate strengths and limitations relevant to language?
- What kinds of evaluation or workflow design matter for linguistic and SSH research?

## Principal Subtopics

- LLM-assisted linguistic research
- corpus analysis and annotation
- evaluation design for language-centered tasks
- reproducible prompt and workflow method
- responsible use in SSH inquiry

## Major Positions or Debates

- whether application work should prioritize utility, interpretability, or epistemic caution
- whether language-oriented workflows can rely on model fluency without stronger validation
