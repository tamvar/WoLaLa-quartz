## Summary
WoLaLa Wiki is a research wiki about language models and language. It is designed as a curated knowledge space for reading, questioning, comparing, and refining ideas about what language models can do, what they cannot do, how they should be studied, and how they connect to linguistics, philosophy of language, cognitive science, and related fields.

This public subset is meant to be read as an introduction to a growing body of source-grounded work on meaning, linguistic structure, mechanistic explanation, and the interpretation of language-model behavior.

## What WoLaLa Wiki Is
The central focus of WoLaLa Wiki is the relation between language models and language.

That includes questions such as:
- what kinds of linguistic structure are present in model representations
- what evidence supports claims about competence, understanding, or limitation
- how meaning, reference, grounding, and language use should be understood in relation to language models
- how neural mechanisms and circuit-level explanations bear on language-related behavior
- how language models connect to theoretical linguistics, philosophy of language, cognitive science, neuroscience, and broader SSH inquiry

WoLaLa Wiki is not just a catalog of papers. It is meant to become a cumulative, interpretable knowledge base whose pages can be read directly, queried, refined, and connected to one another over time.

## How to Read This Site
The public site is organized around a few kinds of pages that work together.

- Overview pages map a topic and orient the reader.
- Concept pages stabilize recurring ideas and distinctions across sources.
- Analysis pages work through reusable questions, comparisons, and disputes.
- Source pages anchor claims in specific papers or source bundles.

If you want orientation, begin with an overview. If you want to understand a recurring idea, move into a concept page. If you want to check how several sources bear on the same question, open an analysis page. If you want to verify a claim at its source, open the linked source page.

## About the Public Subset
This public homepage presents a selective, durable subset of the larger WoLaLa Wiki. It is intended to be readable on its own, without the planning pages, intake pages, or operational logs that support the ongoing curation process behind the scenes.

The current public release is still partial. It opens with three topic bundles that already form a coherent public-facing introduction, while the larger wiki continues to grow behind the scenes.
