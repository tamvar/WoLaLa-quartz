## Summary
Gauthier et al. present SyntaxGym, an online platform for targeted evaluation of language models on controlled linguistic test suites. The paper matters for WoLaLa because it turns syntax-sensitive evaluation from an isolated paper practice into reusable infrastructure.

## WoLaLa Relevance
This source primarily supports [[../overviews/Applications and Best Practices Overview|Applications and Best Practices]] and secondarily [[../overviews/Linguistic Competence and Limitations Overview|Linguistic Competence and Limitations]]. Its contribution is infrastructural rather than theoretical. It gives researchers a common platform for running controlled linguistic evaluations, which is exactly the kind of methodological support strand 6 needs. In relation to strand 1, it helps make competence claims more testable and comparable across models.

## Limitation Or Open Question
SyntaxGym is only as good as the test suites it hosts. It improves comparability and reuse, but it does not by itself settle which constructions are most theoretically revealing or how far syntax-focused tests should generalize to broader language understanding claims.
