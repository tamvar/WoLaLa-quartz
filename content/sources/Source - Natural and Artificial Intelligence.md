## Summary
Francesco Abbate asks how far ChatGPT's linguistic performance should count as intelligent behavior and argues for a carefully limited answer. The paper accepts that artificial systems can display a kind of adaptive linguistic effectiveness, but insists that symbol grounding, environmental coupling, intersubjectivity, and self-development remain major obstacles to treating present systems as genuinely human-like intelligences. For WoLaLa, the paper is a useful contemporary philosophy bridge connecting grounding, decoupling, and comparative cognition.

## WoLaLa Relevance
The source is helpful because it does not collapse into either triumphalist AI rhetoric or a flat denial that current systems exhibit anything interesting. Abbate uses ChatGPT as a case for distinguishing effective linguistic performance from richer cognitive capacities tied to grounding, embodiment, and intersubjective development. That makes the paper a good bridge between present LLM disputes and older philosophy-of-mind arguments about symbols, understanding, and mindedness.

## Key Points
- The paper starts from a behavioral notion of intelligence but argues that successful task performance is not enough for full cognition.
- ChatGPT is treated as behaviorally relevant in language without being intentional in the strong human sense.
- Symbol grounding remains central: text manipulation without stable environmental anchoring is not yet understanding.
- Abbate connects grounding to self-awareness, intersubjectivity, and intercorporeality rather than to a simple sensor attachment fix.
- The paper gives a contemporary version of a familiar WoLaLa distinction between linguistic success and broader mindedness.

## Limitation Or Open Question
The paper is philosophical rather than empirical, and some of its positive suggestions remain programmatic. It is most useful as a conceptual framing source. The open question is how much of the missing grounding and sociality could in principle be approximated by future artificial systems and how much reflects a deeper limit of current language-model architecture.
