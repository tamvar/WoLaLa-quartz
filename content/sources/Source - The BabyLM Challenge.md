## Summary
Alex Warstadt, Leshem Choshen, Aaron Mueller, Adina Williams, Ethan Wilcox, and Chengxu Zhuang's "Call for Papers -- The BabyLM Challenge: Sample-efficient pretraining on a developmentally plausible corpus" introduces a shared task built around a deliberately small training-data regime meant to approximate the scale of linguistic input available to children. For WoLaLa, the source matters because it turns an often vague acquisition comparison into an operational benchmark program: models are compared under strict data budgets and then evaluated with both general NLP tasks and more linguistically targeted probes.

## Limitation Or Open Question
This is a challenge description rather than a mature synthesis of results. It defines an evaluation program and motivates its constraints, but it does not by itself establish which training strategies best model child language learning or whether benchmark gains correspond to stronger theoretical adequacy.
