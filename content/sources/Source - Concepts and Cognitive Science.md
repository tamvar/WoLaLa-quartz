## Summary
Laurence and Margolis provide a high-level survey and critical evaluation of major theories of concepts, including feature-based, theory-based, and atomistic approaches. They emphasize that disputes about concepts span philosophy, psychology, and linguistics, and they ultimately defend a pluralistic picture in which concepts connect to multiple kinds of structure rather than one uniform format. For WoLaLa, the chapter matters as broad background on what conceptual structure theories are available when claims about language, thought, and representation are made.

## Limitation Or Open Question
This is a broad survey chapter rather than a source focused on language models or one sharply bounded theory. The open question is which of its concept theories will prove most useful as the wiki's cognition and reverse-engineering strands become more specific.
