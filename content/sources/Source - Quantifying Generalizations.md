## Summary
Claudia Collacciani, Giulia Rambelli, and Marianna Bolognesi examine how human judgments and LLM behavior compare on quantification, using that contrast to probe a narrower but important kind of linguistic generalization. The source’s central contribution for WoLaLa is to make the abstract debate about `generalization` more specific: instead of asking whether models are broadly human-like, it asks whether they track a linguistically meaningful phenomenon in the same way humans do.

That makes it a good middle term between overtly polemical theory claims and the existing deep literature on probing and competence.

## Limitation Or Open Question
A quantification-focused comparison is valuable precisely because it is narrow, but that also limits what follows from it. Even a strong divergence would not settle the whole relation between human linguistic theory and LLMs, while a strong convergence would still leave open whether the same representations or explanatory resources are involved.
