## Summary
Vid Kocijan, Ernest Davis, Thomas Lukasiewicz, Gary Marcus, and Leora Morgenstern's "The Defeat of the Winograd Schema Challenge" reviews the history of the Winograd Schema Challenge and argues that high benchmark scores did not deliver the intended proof of commonsense reasoning. The paper is important for WoLaLa because it shows how a language-centered benchmark can be overtaken by shortcutting, surrogate-task optimization, and weak evidential standards while still being widely cited as a milestone.

## Limitation Or Open Question
The article is a retrospective critique of one benchmark lineage. It does not show that commonsense reasoning is impossible for current models, and it does not itself provide a replacement evaluation framework. The open question is which richer task designs can test commonsense and discourse reasoning without repeating the same shortcut vulnerabilities.
