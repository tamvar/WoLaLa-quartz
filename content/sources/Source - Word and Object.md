## Summary
W. V. O. Quine’s *Word and Object* is a foundational philosophy-of-language source on reference, meaning, and the relation between linguistic behavior and world-directed interpretation. In WoLaLa terms, its central contribution is to supply historical background for current disputes about whether meaning should be treated as referential, inferential, behavioral, or underdetermined by the available evidence. It therefore does not enter the wiki as generic canon, but as a compact historical support for the already active meaning/reference strand and the newer historical backbone.

## WoLaLa Relevance
This source primarily supports strand 9 and secondarily strand 3. It helps the historical overview connect directly back to current notes on reference and meaning rather than remaining a detached background shelf. It is especially useful where the wiki now needs to show that contemporary LLM debates over reference and semantics inherit older disputes about how linguistic interpretation is fixed and what evidence could ever settle it.

## Limitation Or Open Question
The main caution is that Quine’s framework is not a model-specific intervention. It clarifies conceptual stakes, but it does not itself decide whether current language models refer, understand, or only reproduce patterns. The open question is which Quinean pressures remain most useful for interpreting distributional and LLM-based semantics.
